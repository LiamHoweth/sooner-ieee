# Officer guide: news posts and the inbox

The admin area lives at **/admin.html** on the website. It doesn't appear in the menu. Bookmark it.

## Signing in

- **New officers:** an owner sends you an invite link. Open it, choose your username and password,
  and you're signed in. Each link works once and expires after 7 days. If yours has expired, ask for
  a new one.
- If an owner made your account with a temporary password instead, sign in with it, then change your
  password under **My account**.
- "Keep me signed in" lasts 30 days on that device. Only use it on your own phone or laptop.
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot your password?** for an email link. If email isn't set up,
  ask an owner to reset it.

## Writing a news post

Posts appear on the site's **News** page.

1. Go to **News posts → New post**.
2. Add a **title** and a one-line **summary**. The summary shows on the News page and the home page.
3. Build the post from **blocks**. Add them at the bottom, and reorder them with the arrows.

   | Block | Use it for |
   |---|---|
   | Text | Paragraphs. `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `[link text](https://…)`, lines starting with `- ` for bullets |
   | Heading | Breaking a long post into sections |
   | Image | One photo: text width, wide, or full screen |
   | Gallery | Several photos as a grid or a swipeable carousel |
   | Quote | A line from a speaker or member |
   | Button | A sign-up form, RSVP or application link |
   | Callout | A highlighted box: deadlines, "bring your laptop" |
   | Video | A YouTube or Vimeo link |
   | Stats | Big numbers: "40 new members", "3 podiums" |
   | Event | Date, time, location and RSVP link for a single event |
   | Two columns | Side-by-side text |
   | Divider | A visual break |

4. In **Settings**, choose a category, an accent colour (OU crimson, IEEE blue or storm), and a
   cover image.
5. Click **Preview** to see exactly what visitors will see.
6. Click **Publish**. The post is live straight away. **Save draft** keeps it private.

More options:

- **Schedule:** set a future **Publish date**, then click Publish. The post appears at that time.
- **Hide after:** for deadlines and one-day events. After that time the post leaves the News and
  home pages on its own, and shows as **Expired** in the admin list. Its link still works for anyone
  who already has it. Clear the field to bring it back.
- **Pin:** pinned posts stay at the top of the News page.
- **Unpublish:** open the post and click **Unpublish**. It goes back to being a draft.
- **Edit later:** published posts keep their original date when you update them.

## Photos

- Upload straight from your phone or laptop. Large photos are resized automatically.
- Always fill in **Describe the image** (for example, "Officers at the fall involvement fair").
  Screen readers read it aloud.
- Only post photos of people who are okay being online. Never post student IDs, emails or
  anything personal.
- Images are reused from the library, so you don't need to upload the same logo twice.

## Inbox

Every officer shares one **Inbox**, with the newest items first. It holds two kinds of item:
- messages from the **Contact** page form
- applications from the **Sponsors** page form

The number on the Inbox button counts unread messages plus applications still marked **New**. If the
webmaster has set up email, the officers on the alert list also get an email for each one.

- **Open / Archived:** Open is what still needs attention. Archived holds archived messages, and
  applications marked Accepted or Declined.
- **Show:** filter to messages only, one message topic (Sponsorship, Membership, Events, Press,
  Other), or sponsor applications only.

**Messages**
1. Open a message to read it. That marks it read for everyone, and records who read it.
2. Click **Reply by email**. It opens your email app with the subject filled in. Use **Mark unread**
   if you're leaving it for someone else.
3. Click **Archive** once it's handled. **Move to Open** brings it back.

**Sponsor applications**
1. Open one to see the contact details and what they're interested in.
2. Reply by email, then set the status to **Contacted**.
3. Use **Officer notes** for anything the next person should know. Notes are private.
4. Mark it **Accepted** or **Declined** when it's decided. It moves to Archived.
5. **Export applications (CSV)** downloads every application for a spreadsheet or the treasurer.

Only owners can delete messages or applications.

## Adding officers (owners)

1. Go to **Admins → Invite officer**.
2. Optionally add their name and Gmail, and choose **Admin** or **Owner**.
3. Click **Create invite link**, then **Copy link**. Send it to them privately, for example by text
   or direct message. The link is only shown once.
   - If site email is set up, you can tick the box to email it to them as well.
4. **Waiting to be accepted** lists open invites. Use **Cancel invite** if you sent one to the
   wrong person.

You can still create an account with a temporary password using **Add with a password**.

## Ground rules

- Everything you publish is public and represents OU and IEEE. When in doubt, ask the president.
- Never share your password. Every officer gets their own account, and the activity log records
  who did what.
